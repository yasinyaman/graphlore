    Legend

    Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, drag a node to move it. Click a bubble to open the community, click a node for its details, double-click it for its neighbourhood: what uses it on the left, what it uses on the right. ? lists the keys. The address bar keeps the view, so a link opens it again. G lists what graphlore's tools found (cycles, shared state, surprises, unreferenced code), I shows what depends on the selected node. Made by graphlore from graph.json.

    Open a graph

    Drop a graph.json here, or choose one (graphlore's or graphify's; with its .graphify_labels.json too, for the community names):

    The file is read by this page, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded: the page is not allowed to make any request.
